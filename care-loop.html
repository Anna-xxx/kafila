Kafila · Fatima’s case, step by step

One result. Six steps, each with a responsible person.

Follow Fatima, 52, through six steps, from her positive stool test to the colonoscopy report that closes her case. See who is responsible, what happens next and how Kafila handles delays. After the six steps, three more scenes: the daily programme review, what happens if something goes wrong, and the emergency rule. Fatima is a composite persona; all people and clinics are fictional.

Daily programme review

If something goes wrong

    Emergency, at any moment

    First: “Call 998 now for an ambulance”, a fixed screen, no AI. Separately: her care team is alerted and a doctor calls her the same day. The 998 button is on every screen of her app.

    Rules decide. AI explains. People sign.A rules table sets the deadlines. AI answers patient questions from prepared texts (pending clinical review) and, in the pilot, drafts referrals for a doctor to check. Only clinicians accept, sign or close.
    A responsible person and a deadline at every stepLate, unanswered, declined and urgent cases go to the top of the coordinator's list.
    Closed only with evidenceThe colonoscopy report, or a reason signed by the doctor if it did not happen. Never by AI.

    Prototype with synthetic data. People and clinic names are fictional. Pilot: positive stool test → colonoscopy, one Abu Dhabi provider network, 12 weeks.
