Kafila قافلة · Architecture

Built around existing care systems. Designed to close the gaps between them.

Kafila works alongside existing clinical systems rather than replacing them.

A first pilot can start with a secure daily file of positive screening results and coordinator-assisted booking. Kafila tracks each case, assigns responsibility, monitors deadlines and records the follow-up outcome.

Deeper links with the clinic's medical record, Malaffi, insurers and Sahatna can be added once the interfaces and approvals are in place.

01Demo and pilot

What runs today, and what changes in a pilot.

The demo today

One browser, invented data

Everything runs in one browser. The "lab" is a file of 220 invented results. The patient tab and the clinic tab share the browser's storage, so a tap on her phone shows up at once for Noura. WhatsApp, SMS, booking and insurance are drawn. The only server is our Cloudflare Worker for the AI chat: Gemini through the Worker, and prepared answers whenever AI is off.

The pilot

Connected to the clinic's systems

Results arrive from the lab and patient details come from the clinic record. Updates go back to it once the record is connected. Real WhatsApp, SMS and calls through licensed providers. Storage, AI and speech hosted in the UAE.

The same in both

The same safety principles

The rules table, the red-flag list, the prepared answer library (pending clinical review) and the safety tests carry into the pilot. A real deployment adds secure hosting, sign-in, audit trails, integration monitoring and clinical validation by the partner.

02How it connects

Seven steps to connect one clinic group.

StepWhat happensWho does itStandard
1. AgreementThe provider network signs a data-sharing agreement and names a clinical leadPartner + KafilaContract, ADHICS
2. Results inThe clinic's IT team adds one rule to its integration engine: send a copy of every positive screening result to Kafila. To start, a protected file once a day is enoughPartner ITHL7 v2, or secure file (SFTP)
3. Patient details inKafila reads only what it needs: mobile number, preferred language, family doctor and clinic, insurance planPartner ITFHIR / HL7
4. Updates backThe referral, "appointment booked" and the clinician's closure note go back into the record. Clinicians see Kafila as a worklist next to their own systemKafilaFHIR
5. Messages to the patientWhatsApp from the clinic's verified business number, SMS through a UAE-licensed operator, calls through a local telecom provider. No medical words, only a linkKafila + providersWhatsApp Business Platform, TDRA rules
6. Done elsewhereThrough the partner's official connection, Kafila can see in Malaffi that the colonoscopy was done at another clinic, and the case can close with that evidencePartner + DoHMalaffi interface · with partner
7. StorageEverything is stored and processed on servers in the UAEKafilaFederal Law No. 2 of 2019

What the partner's IT team has to do: one outbound rule in the integration engine (or a daily file), one read / write connection to the record, and staff sign-in through the clinic's own login. No new hardware. The pilot can start with steps 1–5 and 7. A daily file is enough for positive stool tests only. Urgent results, like a blood pressure of 185/115, need a same-day feed and a clinician on call; without that they stay out of the live pilot.

04Safety and privacy

Six rules built into the design.

Rules set urgency

Zone, deadline, red flags, escalation and closure come from a versioned rules table. No AI output can change them. AI may flag a concern for a person to review; only the rules and authorised clinicians set urgency.

Works without AI

Every AI call has a prepared fallback answer. The pilot can run with AI switched off.

A responsible person at every step

Every positive result is one case with a named responsible person and a deadline on each step, until a clinician closes it with evidence. If an expected event does not arrive (no reply, no booking, no report), the case goes to the top of a list.

No health words in messages

WhatsApp and SMS messages, urgent ones included, say only that her clinic needs to reach her, plus a link. The result appears only after an identity check. Consent, opt-outs and every contact attempt are logged.

Minimum data to AI

Kafila does not send her name, Emirates ID, phone, address or record number to the model. She could still type personal details into a free question, so the pilot adds a filter that removes them first. Red-flag questions never reach the AI.

Designed to stay in the UAE

Hosting, database, backups, logs, AI and speech, as the health-data law requires. The whole chain, including message providers and support access, is checked before real patients.

05AI

Which AI, and where it runs.

Demo

Gemini through our Worker

The patient AI chat can call Gemini through our Cloudflare Worker. The code is ready, but AI is off until the key is entered, so today prepared answers from the library reply. This is acceptable only because all data is invented.

Pilot

A model hosted in the UAE

An in-region model with no training on our data and no retention: a large cloud provider's model in a UAE region, or an Arabic-first model such as Jais or Falcon. The model is chosen with the partner and tested on our 50-question safety set.

Pilot

Drafts for staff

Case summary and referral letter drafts, built from case fields. Every draft is checked by code, marked "AI draft · check before use", and a person accepts, edits or ignores it. In the demo these drafts are templates.

Arranged with the pilot partner and DoH, not needed to start: Malaffi access, insurer eligibility checks, the in-UAE AI model, ADHICS control mapping, the Sahatna interface.
